eCommerce Engine for Odoo 20

The shared foundation for Odoo eCommerce connectors

eCommerce Engine is the base module behind our eCommerce connectors for Odoo 20. It is required by the Shopify, WooCommerce, BigCommerce, PrestaShop, and Magento connectors, and is added to your cart automatically when you purchase one of them.

⚠️

Heads up: this module does not connect to any store on its own. It is the engine behind our connectors, so choose the connector for your platform in the Connectors Built on This Engine section below. Installing the engine alone will not synchronize products, orders or inventory.

One Engine, Every Connector

eCommerce Engine contains the logic every Odoo eCommerce connector needs, so each connector only has to handle the platform-specific parts. The result is consistent behavior, one place to monitor all your stores, and faster support when something goes wrong.

Store Account Management

One consistent place to create and configure store accounts, including credentials, company, warehouse, pricing and tax defaults, for every connector.

Sync Logs & Error Tracking

Every import and export is logged with its status and error details, so failures are easy to find and retry.

Queued Background Jobs

Long-running syncs run through scheduled actions and queues, so they don't block users or hit timeouts.

Shared Mapping Models

Common models for products, variants, customers, orders and stock, so every connector maps data the same way.

How it works

1. Purchase a Connector

Pick the connector for your platform from the Odoo Apps Store. eCommerce Engine is added to your cart automatically.

2. Configure Your Store

Add your store account and defaults, following the connector's setup guide.

3. Sync and Monitor

Run synchronization and track every job, log and error from the sync logs.

Functional Q&A

Quick answers to the most common questions about eCommerce Engine.

1 "Can I use eCommerce Engine on its own?" NO
No. The engine does not connect to any eCommerce platform by itself. It provides the shared foundation used by the connectors, so you need the connector for your platform to synchronize products, orders or inventory.
2 "Is it added automatically when I purchase a connector?" YES
Yes. eCommerce Engine (Version 20.0) is added to your cart automatically when you purchase a connector. Please do not remove it, as the connector will not work without it.
3 "Do I pay extra for the engine?" NO
No. The base module's LOC is already included in the connector, so there is no separate charge for the lines of code in the engine.
4 "Does the engine contain platform-specific features like product or order sync?" NO
No. Platform-specific features, supported operations and limitations are part of each connector. Check the Odoo Apps page of the connector you plan to use for its full feature details.
